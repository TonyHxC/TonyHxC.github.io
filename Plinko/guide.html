How to play

Drop balls through a field of pegs and reach each floor's score target before you run out of balls. Clear the floor, pick an upgrade, and climb as high as you can. When you miss a target, the run ends.

  • Mouse / touch: move to aim, click or tap to drop.
  • Keyboard: ← → (or A D) to aim, Space or Enter to drop.
  • Smack (with the ✋ Smack upgrade): swipe your cursor or finger through a falling ball. It flies the way you swiped, and the faster the swipe, the harder the hit. Slow contact does nothing, and a gold ring shows when you're close. On touch screens a quick tap drops a ball; a swipe smacks.
  • Customize Ball (main menu): paint your own ball design with brushes, colors, Fill and Mirror. It's saved on your device and spins as it rolls.
  • ☰ button or Esc pauses to the main menu. ♪ turns sound on or off.

Every peg a ball touches adds points (1 per hit to start). When the ball lands, its points are multiplied:

Ball score = peg points × ball multiplier × bucket multiplier

You get 5 balls per floor. Floor 1 needs 140 points, and each floor needs about 27% more than the last (floor 5 ≈ 420, floor 10 ≈ 1,390). Every 5th floor is a boss floor.

Pegs

Special pegs are where big scores come from. Each one triggers once per ball.

PegWhat it does
Normal+1 point per hit (more with Heavy Ball)
GoldTriple points, +1 coin, and electrifies the ball (up to ⚡5). A charged ball sends lightning chaining up the pegs when it lands
RedDoubles that ball's multiplier. Reds stack: ×2, ×4, ×8…
BombInstantly scores every peg nearby for that ball
SplitterSplits the ball in two, sharing its points so far (max 8 clones)

Fire! A ball whose multiplier reaches ×4 catches fire, at ×8 it burns blue, and at ×16 it becomes a purple inferno. The bucket erupts in a column of fire when a burning ball lands in a ×4 or bigger bucket, or when any ball scores at least 50% of the floor's target. Bigger scores make bigger eruptions, and a ball that clears the floor on its own erupts blue.

Every floor starts with 3 gold pegs and 1 red peg. Bombs and splitters only appear once you take their upgrades.

Between floors

Once you hit the target, the floor ends when your last ball lands. Unused balls are worth coins, so stop dropping once you're there.

Coins you earn

  • +3 for clearing the floor
  • +2 for every ball you didn't use
  • +1 for each gold peg a ball hits
  • Interest, if you own that upgrade

Your choices. You're offered 3 upgrades. Pick one, or:

  • Reroll for a new set of 3 (2 coins, then +1 more each reroll on the same floor)
  • +1 ball next floor for 6 coins (you can buy several)
  • Skip the upgrade and take 4 coins instead

Tips

  • Aim for the edges. The ×8 buckets pay 20 times the middle ×0.4. Edge Lord and Lucky Bucket make edge shots even better.
  • Red pegs are the biggest multiplier. One doubles the ball's score; two make it ×4. Red Pegs is the strongest early pick.
  • Points before multipliers. Heavy Ball and Bomb Pegs raise the base number every multiplier works on.
  • Smack late. A smack near the bottom rows can steer a ball from a ×0.4 bucket into a ×2. Save it for the last few rows.
  • Save your balls. Each unused ball is worth 2 coins.
  • Buy balls before bosses. A +1 ball buy helps going into floors 5, 10 and 15, especially against Lean Times.
  • Your best run is saved on the start screen, so you know what to beat.
