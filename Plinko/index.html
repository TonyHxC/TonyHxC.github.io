How to Play

Drop balls through the pegs. Every peg hit adds points, then the bucket multiplies them. Hit the target before you run out of balls to climb to the next floor and pick an upgrade.

Peg: +points per hit
Gold peg: triple points, +1 coin
Red peg: doubles that ball's multiplier
Bomb peg: triggers every peg nearby
Splitter peg: splits the ball in two
Electric peg: charges the ball; it shoots lightning when it lands

Move to aim, click or tap to drop. Keyboard: ← → and Space.
With the ✋ Smack upgrade, swipe your cursor through a falling ball to hit it. Swipe faster to hit harder.
Hit the target with balls to spare and you choose: keep dropping for more score, or move on and get +2 ● per unused ball.
Every 5th floor is a boss with a nasty twist. Floor 5 is the Hoard Dragon: land balls on his head before he moves. Beat him to unlock gold pegs. Floor 10 is the Hydra: hit its beating heart while its heads bite your balls.
More power-ups unlock after floors 5, 10 and 15. Bomb pegs come after floor 10, electric pegs after floor 15.
