How to Play

Drop balls through the pegs. Every peg hit adds points, then the bucket multiplies them. Hit the target before you run out of balls to climb to the next floor and pick an upgrade.

Peg: +points per hit
Gold peg: triple points, +1 coin
Red peg: doubles that ball's multiplier
Bomb peg: triggers every peg nearby
Splitter peg: splits the ball in two

Move to aim, click or tap to drop. Keyboard: ← → and Space.
With the ✋ Smack upgrade, swipe your cursor through a falling ball to hit it. Swipe faster to hit harder.
Every 5th floor is a boss with a nasty twist.
